# Decision: Regulated Trade Businesses Are First-Class Providers

**Decision date:** 2026-08-25  
**Decision status:** LOCKED  
**Charter version introduced:** 1.1.0  
**Production effect:** NONE  
**Production payment creation:** FROZEN

## Decision

Regulated trade businesses are first-class HustleXP providers in an `ACTIVE_GATED` marketplace lane.

They are not referral-only providers and not a future-maybe category. HustleXP may actively capture regulated demand, recruit and onboard trade businesses, verify credentials, route category-eligible opportunities, coordinate diagnostics and estimates, provide Provider OS, record outcomes and reputation, and retain the customer/provider relationship.

Only the exact capability lacking authority remains disabled. Live marketplace payment, change-order payment, recurring payment, and related money capabilities activate per category only after the required provider credentials, HustleXP legal/compliance policy, candidate-processor category and MCC approval, approved merchant topology and fee mechanism, certification, independent review, and protected release gates pass.

## Provider classes

- `GENERAL_SERVICE_PROVIDER`: eligible individuals and service businesses for approved general-service categories.
- `VERIFIED_TRADE_BUSINESS`: licensed or registered businesses for regulated categories, with task-specific eligible workers or crews.

Both classes share the HustleXP transaction network, Provider OS, evidence, reputation, support, reconciliation, and retention systems.

## Commercial role

The target is a managed marketplace. The Verified Trade Business is the service contractor: it diagnoses regulated scope, bids and contracts for the work, obtains permits, performs through qualified workers, and owns workmanship and warranties.

HustleXP is the marketplace, transaction network, software/operator layer, and disclosed fee recipient. HustleXP must not accidentally present itself as the construction contractor or general contractor. A different role requires a deliberate founder decision plus legal, licensing, insurance, contract, operational, and financial authority.

Qualified referral remains available as an interim capability or measured acquisition experiment. It is not the permanent trade business model.

## Legal and credential boundary

Google and other public reputation signals source prospects. They do not authorize regulated work. Official registration, licensing, certification, bond, insurance, workers' compensation, specialty, restriction, jurisdiction, named-worker, and task records determine eligibility.

Washington L&I states that its Verify tool can expose contractor registration, owner identity, workers' compensation, citations, bond claims, and tradesperson certification. L&I also states that electrical contracting businesses need an electrical contractor license and that the work must be performed by properly certified electricians. Washington's contractor definition includes entities that undertake, offer to undertake, or submit bids for covered work, and includes a consultant acting as a general contractor.

Primary sources:

- [Washington L&I — Verify a Contractor, Tradesperson or Business](https://www.lni.wa.gov/licensing-permits/contractors/hiring-a-contractor/verify-contractor-tradesperson-business)
- [Washington L&I — Electrical Contractor](https://www.lni.wa.gov/licensing-permits/electrical/electrical-licensing-exams-education/electrical-contractor)
- [RCW 18.27.010 — Definitions](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=18.27.010)
